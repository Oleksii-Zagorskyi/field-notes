# Field Notes

Field notes from putting AI to work in a real business — what I ran into and what I took from it. They're bounded by the jobs I've actually faced, and I don't claim anything beyond that. I share them in case they're useful to someone doing similar work — not to persuade anyone of anything.

## The notes

Two folders, two kinds.

- [`patterns/`](patterns/) — things I run daily, written as patterns: the shape and the reasoning, for building your own version.
- [`case-studies/`](case-studies/) — one real piece of work each: what I ran and what came of it.

## Who I am

I'm Oleksii Zagorskyi. I co-own a business of more than 800 people and put AI to work in the parts of it that can be. I've built software for more than twenty years, and I direct agents to build it now — but I've never treated software as the point. It's one lever among many on a business result, and my work is judging which lever actually moves it. These notes are from the times AI was the lever worth pulling.
